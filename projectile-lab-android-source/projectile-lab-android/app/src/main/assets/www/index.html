1. Why is 45° special?
For equal launch and landing heights without drag, 45° gives the greatest range. Try 20°, 45°, then 70°.
2. Independent x and y motion
Gravity changes vertical velocity, but not horizontal velocity when air resistance is off. Watch the vectors.
3. What does air resistance do?
Turn drag on and compare the ideal path with the real path. Faster, larger objects lose more energy to drag.
